# L-TOWN — Lore

## Setting

3049. Humans never touch alien ground — bodies can't survive those environments. Robots do the mining, soldiering, and research. Operators pilot them from stations in orbit.

## The RX-11

- One chassis for everything: army, miner, researcher. Same hull, same speed, same wiring.
- An inert nanote shell. A core gives it life. Each core is a perfect pyramid suspended in nanite fluid — a floating heart. Insert it, and the shell takes the core's form and identity.
- No variants, no upgrades — fixed by arena charter so only cores compete.
- Hull = nanite count: fuel, function, and mass at once. Fire, jumps, and shields spend nanites. Survive 7 seconds of calm — no hits taken, no nanites spent — and the core starts rebuilding the shell, 1 hull per second — 3× faster crouched behind cover. Any spend or wound restarts the clock. Destroyed swarms reprint whole after a 7-second reprint delay.
- Destroyed swarms eject a nanite cache (+100 hull) that nearby shells can siphon — salvage.

## The Atma Cores

Swap the core, change the soul. This season the trials are a customs showcase — only the eleven Maker cores are legal, no vanilla. Each core holds a mind-copy of its Maker's founder; copies glitch, and the glitch is the power. Submissions open yearly.

- **Telepotu** — swap with a random enemy within 120u.
- **Chumantr** — full ghost 10s: move only, no shoot/Q/R/Super.
- **Denja** — 2x speed, free, caps at 8s.
- **Mednix** — heal 1–50 hull, random, self only, capped at full.
- **Tank** — half damage 8s, full speed.
- **Anchor** — one 3s immunity cell, reusable every 40s, zero hull cost.
- **Surge** — rip 30 hull off nearest enemy within 40u, keep 15.
- **Jinx** — dead-man overload: on death, deal 80 to the killer within 60u.
- **Gambler** — equal thirds: heal +200, teleport onto enemy, or instant death.
- **Parasite** — drain 8/s off all strangers within 15u for 6s, keep half; kin immune.
- **Berserker** — +50% damage, +25% speed 8s, then −50 burnout.

Fizzle rule: no valid target means the glitch fires empty — full cooldown, no refund.

## L-Town

A decommissioned Meridian transit hub kept as a battleground — the soil where the first shell was ever printed and linked from orbit. Nobody lives there; only shells and drones touch the ground. Meridian hosts neutrally: keeps the printers, refs the matches, fields no core.

- One map per day — everyone fights the same town; the arena regenerates each dawn.
- 10-minute trials, dawn to dusk, instant requeue. Highest score wins.
- Makers prove their cores here; winning pilots sign with their core as a package. Army clearance pays the most.
- Drones track every unit for the live leaderboard; frags are frags, no bounty. Grounders may die but the show is a job interview: combat is the resume.

## Operators

Open amateurs on scrap rigs — no license, no team, no age gate. Death is a disconnect, not a death: a white-out slam and a headache, then requeue. Most grind for viral fame; the best get signed to pilot army, mining, and research swarms off-world. The crowd loves watching itself.
